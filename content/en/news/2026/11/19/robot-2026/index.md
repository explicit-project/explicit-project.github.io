The EXPLICIT project was presented at the [9th Iberian Robotics Conference (ROBOT2026)](https://robot2026.upc.edu/), held in Barcelona, Spain, from November 18 to 20, 2026. Francisco J. Rodríguez-Lera presented the poster entitled **Self-Explainable Cyber-Physical Systems, Security and Safety in Autonomous Agents (EXPLICIT)**, introducing the project's objectives and ongoing research to the robotics community.

The poster outlined EXPLICIT's approach to improving the security, safety and explainability of cyber-physical systems through operational traceability and LLM-based, human-readable explanations. It presented a unified data model for collecting and retrieving operational evidence from heterogeneous autonomous systems, together with two key components: [ACOLYTE](https://github.com/uleroboticsgroup/ACOLYTE), a tool for extracting and standardising data from different sources, and [BCubed](https://github.com/uleroboticsgroup/BCubed), a blockchain-based library for storing and retrieving standardised records.

The poster also described the experimental evaluation of the prototype in an indoor environment using a RoboCup@Home robot. The experiments explored how operational evidence can support the identification and explanation of anomalous events. The ongoing work focuses on improving storage efficiency and scalability, as well as integrating LLM-based explanations grounded in recorded evidence.

ROBOT2026 is the 9th edition of the Iberian Robotics Conference, bringing together researchers and professionals to share advances in robotics and related fields.
