Researchers from the EXPLICIT project, Adrián Campazas Vega and Claudia Álvarez Aparicio, presented the talk **Robotics and Cybersecurity?** at [AsturCON.tech 2026](https://asturcon.tech/), held in Avilés, Spain, on November 20-21, 2026.

The talk addressed cybersecurity challenges in robotic systems, a topic closely aligned with the EXPLICIT project's research on security, reliability and explainability in autonomous systems. Their contribution provided an opportunity to share the project's research interests with the wider cybersecurity community.

AsturCON.tech is a non-profit cybersecurity conference that brings together professionals, researchers, students and companies from the technology sector. The event is organised by ORICIO.org, a non-profit association that promotes cybersecurity awareness and training, as well as collaboration between academic institutions, businesses and public institutions.
