Vicente Barreiro Serrano and Francisco Javier Rodríguez Lera, researchers at the University of León involved in the EXPLICIT project, presented their work **REGAL: Accessible and Trustworthy Explanations for Robots with ROS** at the [ROSCon España 2026 conference](https://ros.org.es/roscon2026/ROSConES2026.html), held in Valencia, Spain, on October 27-28, 2026.

The presentation introduced REGAL, a ROS 2-based framework designed to make robot communication more accessible through Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) pictograms. The system enables service robots, such as Pepper, to explain their actions using visual symbols rather than relying exclusively on spoken or written language. This approach is particularly relevant for people with autism spectrum disorder, intellectual disabilities, or other conditions that may affect language comprehension.

REGAL uses a two-stage pipeline based on Large Language Models (LLMs) to interpret information about the robot's actions and translate it into pictogram-based messages adapted to each user's cognitive profile. It also incorporates semantic search over the ARASAAC pictogram database and bidirectional integration with AsTeRICS-Grid, an open-source AAC tool.

ROSCon España is a meeting point for developers and professionals working with ROS, bringing together participants with different levels of experience, from beginners to experts, to share knowledge, learn from one another, and exchange ideas within the robotics community. The 2026 edition was held in collaboration with the Open Source Robotics Foundation and organized by the Universitat Politècnica de València, the Instituto de Automática e Informática Industrial (ai2), and the Escuela Técnica Superior de Ingeniería Informática, with local support from Robotnik.
