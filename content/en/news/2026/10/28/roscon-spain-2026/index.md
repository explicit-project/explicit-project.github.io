Vicente Barreiro Serrano and Francisco Javier Rodríguez Lera, researchers at the University of León involved in the EXPLICIT project, presented their work **REGAL: Accessible and Trustworthy Explanations for Robots with ROS** at the [ROSCon España 2026 conference](https://ros.org.es/roscon2026/ROSConES2026.html), held in Valencia, Spain, on October 27-28, 2026.

REGAL addresses the challenge of providing accessible and trustworthy explanations for robotic systems based on the [Robot Operating System (ROS)](https://www.ros.org/). The work explores how explainability can contribute to making robotic systems more transparent and understandable, facilitating interaction between robots and their users while strengthening trust in autonomous robotic technologies.

ROSCon España is a meeting point for developers and professionals working with ROS, bringing together participants of different experience levels, from beginners to experts, to share knowledge, learn from one another, and exchange ideas within the robotics community. The 2026 edition was held in collaboration with the [Open Source Robotics Foundation](https://www.openrobotics.org/) and organized by the [Universitat Politècnica de València](https://www.upv.es/), the [Instituto de Automática e Informática Industrial (ai2)](https://www.ai2.upv.es/), and the [Escuela Técnica Superior de Ingeniería Informática](https://www.upv.es/entidades/etsinf), with local support from [Robotnik](http://robotnik.eu/es/).

The presentation provided an opportunity to share the work developed within the EXPLICIT project with the ROS community and to contribute to ongoing discussions on explainability, transparency, and trust in robotic systems.
